MASIMOUS II - demo v0.1.0 (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

START: double-click MasimousII.exe. Keep the folder "data_MasimousII_windows_x86_64" next to it.
If Windows SmartScreen warns ("unknown publisher"): click "More info", then "Run anyway".

               Keyboard        Xbox controller
Move           A / D           Left stick / D-pad
Jump           Space           A
Attack         J               X
Dodge          L / Shift       B
Parry          I               Y
Heal (flask)   H               LB
Skills         Q / E / R       RB / RT / LT
Talk / rest    W               Up
Backpack       Tab / B         View
Pause / menu   Esc             Menu

Rest at a shrine to save. Settings (window size, fullscreen, volume, brightness, vibration, keys) are in the menu.
